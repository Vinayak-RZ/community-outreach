===== Slide 15 =====
RULE 3
Relative grading within a topic
Your work is compared with the other teams holding the same topic and within the whole batch.
Why compare within a topic
The four teams face the same community and the same difficulty.
It removes the argument that one topic was easier than another.
It rewards depth of engagement, not choice of subject.
What separates the top team
More primary contact — people actually spoken to, on record.
Evidence a sceptic would accept, not photographs of the team.
A question asked that the other three teams did not think of.
Honesty about what failed and what could not be verified.
Duplicate work across the four teams is expected. Duplicate stuff will lead to deductions for all teams.

===== Slide 20 =====
NON-NEGOTIABLE
Consent, dignity and the line you do not cross
The people in these eight topics depend on this campus for their living. A careless report can cost someone a job.
DO
Ask permission before recording, in the language the person speaks.
Anonymise by default — use role and gate number, not names.
Tell people what the work is for and that they may decline.
Share what you found with the people you found it from.
DO NOT
No photograph or quote that could identify a person to their employer.
No promise of a job, money, or a fixed problem that you cannot deliver.
No recording of children, and no household details of anyone's family which is identifiable.
No naming of individual contractors or officials in the poster. Report stuff, but be innovative.

===== Slide 21 =====
LEARN FROM LAST YEAR
Where teams lose marks
The one-day visit
Four hours on a Sunday, thirty photographs, no return visit. Depth shows in the report and its absence shows faster.
The team selfie poster
An A3 sheet that is mostly pictures of the team. The poster is about the community, not about you.
Untestable objectives
“Spread awareness.” Nobody can confirm or deny it, so it earns nothing.
Last-week evidence
Consent sheets signed in bulk on the final day. The dates give it away.
Four identical reports
All four teams on a topic reporting the same surface facts. Nobody in that cluster stands out.
