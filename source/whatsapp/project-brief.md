# Project brief: EEC 399 Community Outreach, "Campus dogs"

Sources: [chat d/m] = raw-transcript.txt (WhatsApp, 2026). [PPTX s#] = course deck, slide number. Nothing here comes from interview recordings yet.

## Course (from the PPTX)
- EEC 399 Community Outreach, Dept. of EE, IIT Roorkee, third-year B.Tech, 2 credits. It's fieldwork, not a literature review: identify a societal problem, work with the people living it, and ask honestly whether technology helps. [PPTX s1-2]
- 168 students, 8 fixed topics, teams of 5-7, at most 4 teams per topic. Topics are drawn at random. [PPTX s3, s14]
- **Relative grading within a topic.** Teams on the same topic are graded against each other. What sets a team apart: more primary contact on record, evidence a sceptic would accept, a question the other teams didn't think of, and honesty about what failed. Duplicate work across teams costs every team marks. [PPTX s15]
- **Topic 3, Campus dogs ("Two sides of one street").** Example question: "What happens when it's not safe to walk in a campus?" Expected strands: population mapping (numbers and territories by zone); the feeders' view; the afraid side (bites, near-misses, routes avoided after dark); facts on record (health-centre bite and vaccination figures, sterilisation/ABC status, existing policy); and a workable coexistence proposal both sides would sign. [PPTX s8]
- **KPI table, five rows with targets you set:** Reach (people engaged; evidence: consent sheet and contact log), Depth (person-hours; dated field diary), Record (a new dataset with a method note), Output (something handed back, e.g. a written submission to an office, with acknowledgement), Change (one concrete shift, confirmed in writing). [PPTX s16-17]
- **Final evaluation:** an A3 poster (printed and PDF), the KPI table with achieved figures, evidence (photos, audio, video, interview records, datasets; geotagged photos and dated videos only), and a group plus individual viva. Claims without evidence count as not done. [PPTX s18]
- **Deadlines:** team and topic were due on the day of the briefing. The final review is "October sometime", with no exact date in the deck or chat. [PPTX s19; chat 12/10 Vrinda: "oct hi bola tha"]
- **Ethics:** ask before recording, anonymise by default (role or gate rather than names), no identifying photos or quotes, no promises, no recording children, no naming contractors or officials on the poster. [PPTX s20]
- **Where teams lose marks:** one-day visits, team-selfie posters, untestable aims like "spread awareness", consent sheets signed in bulk at the end, identical reports, and team members telling different stories in the viva. [PPTX s21]
