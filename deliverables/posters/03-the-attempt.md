# Sheet 03 — The attempt

_Not the sheet to build. The maintained form for the one A3 is [`improve-poster-prompt.md`](improve-poster-prompt.md). That sheet includes the KPI table and the previous campus map._

A3 portrait, 297 × 420 mm. Hung on the right. Slide 8 asks for a coexistence plan both sides would sign. Slide 2 asks whether technology helps, and says an honest no is a result. Slide 21 says an untestable aim such as "spread awareness" earns nothing. This sheet is an attempt the team can defend in a viva. It does not look like a solved policy.

## What the sheet says

**Walk-by:**

Doors, signed zones, a shut boundary.

Barlow Condensed, 52 pt, weight 700. One line. "Doors" is the oxide, `oklch(0.47 0.11 38)`, because the door is the change with the strongest evidence: dogs inside hostels are the most common lived problem, and one bhawan has already seen raised bins work. Source: `analysis/synthesis.md` F2, R1; survey row 112.

Under the walk-by, 14 pt, one line, mute: Not a clearance. Not an app.

## How the page is built

Three bands of different height. They are not three cards. No shared icon, no shared border.

| Band | Height | Job |
|---|---|---|
| Top | 78 mm | What does not transfer onto this campus |
| Middle | 210 mm | The attempt, split into what people can change and what only an office can change |
| Foot | 72 mm | Technology, then the gaps, in smaller type |

Bands are separated by space (14 mm, then 18 mm), not by rules. The foot is smaller on purpose: slide 15 rewards honesty, and honesty should not shout.

### Top band

13.5 pt. Three sentences.

Feeding zones are already here. People describe them as next to garbage, or marked by a board too small to matter, and they feed at rooms anyway. Copying "make a feeding zone" from another campus repeats a rule this campus already does not keep. Source: `analysis/synthesis.md` F5.

A CCF volunteer mentioned meshes on hostel entrances at another IIT, and an Uttarakhand High Court ruling. Both are something a volunteer said. The desk research Jiya asked for on 14 and 15 October is not written up in this repo. The sheet does not summarise a court order. Source: `analysis/interview-coding.md` I23; `project-brief.md` (ideas, 14–15/10); `source/whatsapp/timeline.md`.

26 of 98 written answers still ask for removal of all or most dogs. CCF will not sign that. A sheet that only says "remove" or only says "let them be" fails people we actually spoke to. Source: `analysis/survey-analysis.md` §6.1; `analysis/synthesis.md` F1.

### Middle band

Two columns, unequal again. Left 100 mm, right 152 mm. The office column is wider because that is where the physical work sits. Headings:

- Left: "What we can change without an office"
- Right: "What we are asking an office to change"

Left, three lines, 14 pt:

- Feeders agree to fewer spots, signed, at a fixed hour, away from doors and garbage. Source: `analysis/synthesis.md` R2.
- A specific dog or pack can be reported. The report is not a demand to clear the street. Source: `analysis/synthesis.md` R6.
- First-years can be told how to pass a dog, and feeders can be told why a bowl at a door hurts the dogs' case. This is one line, not the project. Slide 21. Source: `analysis/synthesis.md` R7.

Right, four lines. Each line ends with the office, in mute, on the same line. 14 pt.

- Mesh or a self-closing door at bhawan entrances and messes. Raise or cover the bins. One bhawan already netted a fence. Raised bins stopped dogs entering another. Chief Warden, bhawan wardens, Estate. Source: `analysis/synthesis.md` R1; survey row 112.
- Fewer zones than today, away from paths, doors and garbage, times fixed with the feeders. CCF with Estate. Source: `analysis/synthesis.md` R2.
- Repair the broken gate and the Himalaya-side gaps so outside dogs and dumped puppies are not walking in. This stays labelled a hypothesis until we photograph the gaps. Estate, Security. Source: `analysis/synthesis.md` R3, F4.
- Publish sterilisation and vaccination counts once a year. Put a one-page notice in every bhawan: where to go at night, first dose within two days. That window is from hospital notes, not from a recording. Handle one aggressive dog without culling. The current animal-birth-control rules have not been checked, and the sheet says so. CCF, Security, Dean of Students, institute hospital. Source: `analysis/synthesis.md` R4, R5, R6.

Do not print "we will build the doors." The team is asking. Slide 20: promise no fix you cannot deliver.

### Foot

11 pt for the technology sentence, 10 pt for the gap.

Technology mostly does not help. The changes are doors, bins, walls, zones, and a published count. Two tools only: a form for one aggressive dog or a gap in the boundary, and a shared map of the bad routes. 2 of 98 written answers suggested any technology. Source: `analysis/synthesis.md`, "Does technology help?"

Then the gap, which is part of the result: court research is not written up. The boundary has not been photographed. Consent sheets and a field diary do not exist. Nothing has been handed to an office, and no written commitment exists. Reach figures in `deliverables/kpi-table.md` (211 responses, 36 interviews) are real and are not reprinted here. They were on sheet 01. Person-hours are not measured.

## What the squint test should show

Blurred: one short sentence, a thin top band, a heavy middle with a wider right column, a quiet foot. If the foot is as dark as the middle, the gaps have been designed as a confession graphic. They should read as a footnote that happens to be true.

- A dog-population total, a bite total, or a claim that sterilisation is finished.
- "Spread awareness" as a heading.
- A KPI progress bar. The updated KPI table is its own hand-in (slide 18). This sheet only keeps the failures that change what we are allowed to claim.
- The two remembered stories. They are still not in the record. See sheet 01.

## How the three sheets meet

Pinned with the walk-by baselines on one horizontal line, about 10 mm between sheets.

01 ends on a missing count. 02 ends on the sentence both sides could sign. 03 starts from the rules that already failed and asks for doors, zones, and a boundary. A person who only reads the three walk-by lines still has the argument: managed, not removed; one side stays and the other changes road; the attempt is doors, zones, and a shut boundary.
