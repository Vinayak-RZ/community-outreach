_Not the sheet to build. The maintained form for the one A3 is [`improve-poster-prompt.md`](improve-poster-prompt.md). That sheet includes the KPI table and the previous campus map._

A3 portrait, 297 × 420 mm. Hung on the left. An evaluator in a bright EE corridor should get the sentence without stepping in. The body is for the person who stops.

This sheet answers slide 8's "facts on record" and the population question by saying what we have and what we were refused. It does not open with a story. ## How the page is built

Live area inside 15 mm margins: 267 × 390 mm. The walk-by sits in the top 78 mm, alone, with more air under it than above it. Barlow Condensed, 54 pt, weight 700, ink `oklch(0.24 0.012 55)` on paper `oklch(0.965 0.012 85)`. "181" later is the same family at 64 pt in oxide `oklch(0.47 0.11 38)`.

Below that, two columns that are not a pair of cards. No boxes. A 0.25 mm hairline between them is structural, in the paper's own grey, not a coloured stripe.

| Column | Width | Job |
|---|---|---|
| Left | 168 mm | What people chose, and where the trouble is lived |
| Right | 84 mm | What was asked for and not given |

The right column is narrower and has more empty paper. The gap is the point: the record is thinner than the opinion.

Left column, top to bottom, 14 pt Barlow, 16 mm between blocks, 6 mm inside a block:

1. **181 of 211** chose a limit on where dogs may be: out of hostels 75, designated areas 54, vaccinated or sterilised only 52. **17** chose free movement. Removal was not an option. 7 people typed it under Other. 26 of 98 written answers propose removing all or most dogs. Source: `analysis/synthesis.md` F1, `analysis/survey-analysis.md`.
2. **143 of 211** have had a dog inside a hostel or building. **129** have felt unsafe or been chased. Among those chased, 112 of 129 rated the problem 4 or 5. Among the rest, 18 of 82 did. Source: `analysis/synthesis.md` F2, F3.
3. The campus animal-care group (CCF) feeds at set spots, treats injured animals, and says bites come from a few dogs and from gaps in the boundary. **149 of 211** had not heard of CCF or of organised sterilisation. Source: `analysis/synthesis.md` F4, F6.

Right column, 13 pt, more leading (1.45):

- This team did not count the dogs. A verbal figure from CCF is about a year old and the transcript of the number is garbled. It is not printed. Source: `analysis/synthesis.md` F4, F7.
- The hospital was asked in person and by email. No bite register, vaccination total, or sterilisation record was handed over. Source: `deliverables/kpi-table.md` (Output, Record), `analysis/synthesis.md` F7.
- Sterilisation is in dispute: CCF says almost all females are neutered; guards and a caretaker say litters continue. Both can be partly true if outside dogs are the unneutered ones. The sheet states the dispute in two lines. It does not pick a winner. Source: `analysis/synthesis.md`, conflicts table.

Bottom 36 mm, full width, 12 pt mute: places people named, labelled as names rather than a map. MAC toward Rajiv. MNC toward Govind. SAC. The Mandir. The library route. A broken gate. Gaps on the Himalaya side. Under that, 9 pt: 199 of 211 answers arrived on one day. 209 were students. Say respondents, not campus opinion. Source: `analysis/survey-analysis.md` §6 and §7.

Sheet number "01" is 11 pt, top left, aligned to the same baseline as 02 and 03 so the three sheets read as one row.

## What the squint test should show

Blurred, the page is a large sentence, then one rust-coloured 181, then a heavy left block and a thin right block. If the right block looks as heavy as the left, the layout has failed.
