Other campuses regulate place. The Court split the law in two.

IIT Bombay
3 June 2024: feed only at designated spots, not in hostels or academic buildings. Feeders register. Feeding outside a spot draws a penalty of Rs 500. An April 2026 annexure names 19 spots and says it is interim, while municipal measures under the Supreme Court order of 7 November 2025 are pending.
IIT Kanpur
Office order, 28 January 2025: 12 feeding zones, none in the academic area. Dogs are not permitted in lecture complexes, hall rooms, or children's play areas. Feed elsewhere and the repeat offence removes the person from campus.
Outside India
MIT, Stanford and Harvard ban or license owned pets, allow trained service dogs, and sit inside city leash laws. A loose dog is impounded. They are not a model for an ownerless campus pack. Boğaziçi University, in its own note through February 2023: vaccinate, sterilise, do not feed inside buildings, do not leave outside animals on campus, and send aggressive dogs to animal living areas.
Street
On an ordinary street, the 2023 Animal Birth Control Rules and the Supreme Court order of 22 August 2025 say: capture, sterilise, vaccinate, and release back to the same area. Not if the dog is rabid or suspected rabid, and not if it displays aggressive behaviour.
Institution
On an educational institution, the order of 7 November 2025, affirmed on 19 May 2026: fence the premises, name a nodal officer at the entrance, and have the municipality remove every stray dog found there to a shelter. Not back to that institution.
Not this
Do not say this repeals the street rule. Do not say an aggressive dog may be killed. The Court's later direction stays inside the 2023 Rules, and the retrieved euthanasia rule is for incurably ill and mortally wounded dogs.

A rule we already fail will not start working because another campus printed it.

Feeding zones
Feeding zones are already on this campus. They sit by garbage or behind a small board, and people feed at rooms. Bombay and Kanpur add a sanction, and they keep the spots out of hostels. The spot alone is the part we have already watched fail.
Leash rule
A leash-and-owner rule does not transfer. These dogs do not have an owner to fine.
Sterilised dogs
“Keep the sterilised dogs where they are” was the coexistence most of our respondents would sign: 181 of 211 chose a limit, not removal. Direction E of 7 November 2025, affirmed 19 May 2026, says a dog removed from an institution is not released back there. Repairing the boundary is the part of our own fieldwork the order now requires. Keeping removed dogs on the campus is the part it does not allow.
Clearing every dog
Clearing every dog is what 26 of 98 written answers still ask, and what CCF will not sign. The order gives removal to the municipality, into a shelter. We have not seen a Roorkee shelter arrangement, and we did not retrieve a Uttarakhand High Court compliance order. Do not promise the removal.
