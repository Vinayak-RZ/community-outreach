## Actions that would close the gaps before the review
1. **Consent sheets (Reach).** Re-contact the reachable interviewees (guards, canteen staff, CCF, the students) and get each one to sign a dated consent sheet. Don't sign them in bulk at the end; the deck lists that as a way to lose marks. Note honestly which interviewees couldn't be reached.
2. **Contact log (Reach).** One row per interview: ID (I01–I36), date, place, role, interviewer(s), file, and consent yes/no. It can be built from `analysis/data/interview-codes.csv` and team memory. Only the team can fill in the interviewer column.
3. **Dated field diary with person-hours (Depth).** Rebuild it from the chat and file timestamps: date, place, members present, start and end times. Each member confirms their own entries, and it gets mentor-initialled if a mentor is assigned.
4. **Hotspot map and ear-notch count (Record).** One dated evening walk on 3–4 routes (MAC–Rajiv, MNC–Govind, Himalaya side and gate 7, SAC): geotagged photos, dogs counted, ear-notched or not. This also tests the sterilisation conflict.
5. **Written acknowledgement (Output).** A 2-page findings note to CCF and the Chief Warden (or the Dean of Students), plus the awareness poster placed with CCF, each with a dated email or signed acknowledgement.
6. **Hospital and admin data request (Record, and facts on record).** A short written request to the CMO and the Security office for monthly bite cases and vaccine doses for the last 2 years, and the sterilisation drive records. A refusal is also a result: record it.
7. **One change (Change).** Ask one warden or CCF for a single small commitment that follows from R1 or R2 in `analysis/synthesis.md`, and get it confirmed by email.
