## Change: what would count

The course's Change row asks for "1 stated commitment or corrected practice", confirmed in writing (slide 16), and slide 21 says an aim like "spread awareness" earns nothing. "People became more aware" can't be confirmed or denied. Options that could still be evidenced quickly:
- A **written reply from CCF** to the solutions report, stating anything they will do (even "we will share the feeding-point list with wardens").
- A **warden or bhawan secretary** confirming by email that the poster stays up on the notice board, or that a feeding spot was moved away from a door.
- A **one-question recheck** at 2–3 poster sites ("do you know where the nearest feeding point is?"), compared with the survey's 70.6% who had not heard of CCF. Report it as a small sample.

If none of these happens, the Change row honestly says **"requested, not yet confirmed"**.
