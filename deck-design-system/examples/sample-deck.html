Why it happens

Teams have systems of record. They do not have a system of action that turns the data into a change on the floor.

  • Who has itOperators running high-volume lines
  • Today they useSpreadsheets, charts, and after-the-fact reports
  • What they loseMaterial, energy, and machine hours on every miss
  • Who buysThe owner. Shift leads use it every day.
“I have plenty of data. What I want are actions that move the number.”

How it works

Connect to what the site already records, model each process, and hand the team one change to approve.

01
Connect
Pull machine logs, lab sheets, and scrap registers — paper ones included. No new hardware.
02
Model
Calibrate a predictive model of each process. It keeps learning as the line changes.
03
Act
Your team approves each action. Proven changes keep running inside your limits.
