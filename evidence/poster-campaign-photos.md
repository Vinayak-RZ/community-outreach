## The poster itself (text read from IMG_9787, full resolution)

- Kicker: "You may be standing on one"
- Title: **DOG FEEDING ZONE**. Strap: "Feed here, not everywhere". "You are near one". "IIT Roorkee · 8 designated points"
- Four rules: (1) Feed dogs only at the 8 marked zones. (2) This spot is one of them. (3) Keep it clean. Take the plastic with you. (4) If a dog comes close: stay calm, look away, back away slowly.
- Map with eight numbered points: **1** Govind · DS Barracks, **2** Himalaya · KIH, **3** Sheel Kunj Gate, **4** Sarojini Water Tank, **5** Gate 5 · iHUB (near Kasturba), **6** PNB · Green Gate, **7** MAC Theatre, **8** Cautley Mess. Also labelled: LBS Stadium, Library, Main Building, Ganga Canal. Scale bar 200 m.
- Footer: injured or non-neutered dog → a CCF email address and a phone number. The number is too blurred in the photo to copy reliably; take it from the poster file, not from this note.
- Source of the eight points: **not recorded in the repo.** It is not the same list as `evidence/hotspot-walk-map.md` (which marks only CCF/feeder triangles) or `deliverables/map-plan.md` (two approximate spots). Before the viva, be ready to say where the eight points came from (CCF list? institute list?).

## What this does and does not prove

- **It proves** that the poster exists, what it says, and that copies were up at about eight campus places, with GPS, in the early hours of **24 November 2026**.
- **It does not prove 7–8 November.** The team's list dates the poster drive to 7–8 Nov. No file in this bundle carries a 7–8 Nov date. If earlier photos exist on phones (original files, not WhatsApp copies), add them. Otherwise say in the viva that the dated photos are from 24 Nov.
- **"How people engaged"** is six frames (IMG_9792–9797) of two people reading the handout. The bundle doesn't record who they are. If they are team members, these are team photos, and the course says team photos are not evidence (`course/course-deck.txt`, slides 15 and 21).
- Most frames are team members posing. On the poster and in the slides, use the frames that show the poster in place without people (IMG_9775–9777, IMG_9783–9785, the water-cooler and pillar shots).
- These photos show placement. They don't measure any change in feeding behaviour. See `deliverables/kpi-table.md`, Change row.
