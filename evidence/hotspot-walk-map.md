# Hotspot walk map

Source image for the deck: `deliverables/posters/assets/hotspot-walk-map.jpg`.

## What it is

A team walk map of IIT Roorkee. The date of the walk and who walked are **not recorded**.

## What the marks mean

- **Colour ranks 1 to 3.** How many dogs were seen at that spot on the walk. No counts were kept; the ranks are the walkers' ranking of what they saw.
- **Green triangles.** Feeding spots as told to the team by CCF or feeders. They are not an institute list. They were not checked on the ground for this map.

## Where it disagrees with `deliverables/map-plan.md`

- That file records only two approximate feeding spots (near Mechanical and near Kasturba) and says no feeding spot was named in the interview, survey or CCF sources used for the mention map.
- MAC and MNC are team-confirmed or named hotspots in the map plan, but they are **not marked** on this walk map.
- Punjab National Bank, Civil Department and Azad Bhawan appear on this walk map. They do not appear in the mention data behind `analysis/figures/fig13_hotspot_map_draft.svg`.

## KPI effect

The KPI "Record" row in `deliverables/kpi-table.md` stays **not met**. This walk ranked spots. It did not produce a dog count, an ear-notch check, or a dated census.
